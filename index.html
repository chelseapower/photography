About

Daniel Garriga is a New York photographer born in Barcelona and raised between Berlin and Paris. Daniel trained at NYU’s Tisch School of the Arts and built his career shooting campaigns for brands like Chanel, Oscar de la Renta, Martini, Turkish Airlines, and Sephora.

Daniel brings the same campaign-level storytelling to his luxury architecture photography projects. His hospitality, properties and commercial real estate clients include Cushman & Wakefield, Apple Store on NYC’s Fifth Avenue, Herald Square’s Samsung Store, Arc Linea showrooms, Hilton Resorts and exclusive historic hotels such as The Edison, The Frederick, The Evelyn, The Iroquois and The Belleclaire.

Based in New York. Available to travel.
